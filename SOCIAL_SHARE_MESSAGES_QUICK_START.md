# 📱 Social Share Messages - Quick Start

## ✅ What's New?

You can now customize the WhatsApp and Facebook share messages that appear when attendees share your webinar from Thank You and Countdown pages!

## 🚀 How to Use (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Go to Your Webinar Settings
1. Open **Dashboard → Webinars**
2. Click on your webinar
3. Scroll down to **"📱 Social Share Messages"**

### Step 2: Write Your Messages

**WhatsApp Example:**
```
🎉 I just registered for {{webinarTitle}}!

Join me on {{webinarDate}} at {{webinarTime}}

Register here: {{joinLink}}

See you there! 🚀
```

**Facebook Example:**
```
Just registered for: {{webinarTitle}}
Join me and learn something amazing!
```

### Step 3: Save!
Click **"Save Changes"** at the bottom.

## 🎯 Pro Tips

✅ **Use emojis** to make messages pop (especially for WhatsApp)  
✅ **Keep it short** - 2-4 sentences work best  
✅ **Create urgency** - "Don't miss out!" "Limited spots!"  
✅ **Always include** `{{joinLink}}` so people can register  
✅ **Test it** - Register yourself and click the share buttons  

## 📝 Available Variables

Copy and paste these into your messages:

- `{{webinarTitle}}` - Your webinar name
- `{{webinarDate}}` - The date (e.g., "Monday, December 25, 2025")
- `{{webinarTime}}` - The time (e.g., "7:00 PM EST")
- `{{joinLink}}` - Link to register (**IMPORTANT: Always include this!**)

## 🔄 What Happens If You Leave It Empty?

No problem! If you don't set custom messages, we'll use these defaults:

**WhatsApp Default:**  
"I just registered for '{{webinarTitle}}' happening on {{webinarDate}} at {{webinarTime}}. Join me: {{joinLink}}"

**Facebook Default:**  
"Check out this webinar: {{webinarTitle}}"

## ✨ Where Do These Messages Appear?

Your custom messages will show up when attendees click:
- **WhatsApp share button** on Thank You page
- **Facebook share button** on Thank You page  
- **WhatsApp share button** on Countdown page
- **Facebook share button** on Countdown page

## 🎉 That's It!

You're all set! Your custom share messages will help you get more registrations through word-of-mouth referrals.
